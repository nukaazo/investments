01 The Problem

Local shops already have customers.
They lack the tools.

Ordering, delivery, stock, and retention still live in notebooks, WhatsApp chats, and memory. That makes growth hard and daily work messy.

Order Friction

Online orders

No simple, unified way to take, accept, and track neighbourhood orders without manual calls and lost messages.

Inventory Blindness

Stock clarity

What is available, low, or missing is rarely visible in one place. Stockouts lead directly to lost neighbourhood sales.

Customer Retention

Repeat buyers

Known customers are hard to keep without a digital reorder loop, losing ground to venture-subsidised quick commerce.

ℹ Core product thesis. Merchant interviews confirm ordering, stock visibility, and customer repeat as top paid priorities.

02 The Product

Three apps.
One local business.

Merchant App Shopkeeper

Run the shop

Digital catalogue, live price edits, custom delivery range, delivery fee rules, stock alerts, and invited riders — completely merchant controlled.

  • Under 10-minute shop setup
  • Live inventory and low-stock alerts
  • Direct counter order acceptance
Customer App Shopper

Order nearby

Find nearby shops, see verified inventory and clear transparent charges, and reorder daily staples from neighbourhood shops they already trust.

  • Zero surge markups on store prices
  • Direct shop WhatsApp/chat connection
  • 1-tap repeat grocery baskets
Delivery App Fulfilment

Finish the job

Accept shop invitations, follow clear pickup and bag-check steps, and verify delivery with OTP for seamless counter-to-doorstep handoff.

  • Shop-designated rider invitations
  • Optimised local 1–3 km routes
  • Instant delivery completion proof
ℹ Intended platform architecture. Production code, APIs, and seller apps can be inspected during investor technical diligence.

Appendix Risk & Governance

Disciplined capital,
transparent risks.

Margin Sensitivity Core Margin Protection

Payment gateway fees (~2%) take half of the 4% commission. Mitigation: Direct UPI integrations and shopkeeper wallet settlements reduce transaction overhead.

B2B Exposure Strict Proxy Capital Cap

Wholesale distribution carries working capital risk. Mitigation: Proxy is strictly capped at ₹20L, operating strictly on supplier-direct pre-paid or cash-on-delivery terms.

Downside Scenario Runway Stress Testing

Under a downside stress test (60% active shops, 80% order frequency), month-18 cash remains above ₹8.3L with an emergency hiring freeze trigger.

Financing Timing Follow-on Milestones

Fixed overhead reaches break-even in year 2. Series A fundraising will initiate around Month 12 based on verified cohort retention metrics.
