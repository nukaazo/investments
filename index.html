01 / Problem

Local shops already have customers.
They lack the tools.

Ordering, delivery, stock, and retention still live in notebooks, WhatsApp, and memory. That makes growth hard and daily work messy.

Online orders

No simple way to take and track neighbourhood orders end to end.

Stock clarity

What is available, low, or missing is rarely visible in one place.

Repeat buyers

Known customers are hard to keep without a clear reorder loop.

This is the product thesis. Merchant interviews should confirm which jobs they will pay to solve first.

02 / Product

Three apps.
One local business.

Shopkeeper

Run the shop

Catalogue, prices, delivery range, fees, stock, and invited riders — controlled by the merchant.

Customer

Order nearby

Find local products, see charges clearly, and reorder from shops they already trust.

Delivery

Finish the job

Accept shop invitations, follow pickup steps, and confirm delivery.

Intended platform shape. Production features should be inspected before investor diligence.

Appendix · Risk

The case needs
disciplined capital.

Core margin ₹8 per illustrative ₹500 order before fixed costs. Payment fees can consume half the commission.
Proxy exposure Up to ₹20L capped. Supplier-direct and prepaid first; inventory or credit needs separate approval.
Downside At 60% shops, 80% frequency, ₹450 basket — month-18 cash ~₹8.3L, below the ₹25L reserve.
Follow-on Core still below fixed-cost break-even at month 24. Prepare the next round around month 12.

Reconcile actual costs and tax treatment before presenting this as a financing forecast.
